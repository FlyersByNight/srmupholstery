Services

What we can do for you

Automotive

Car, van, classic and motorcycle seats, headlinings, door cards and full interior re-trims. Leather, vinyl and fabric.

Furniture

About us

Local craftsmanship, honest advice

SRM Upholstery is an independent upholstery workshop based on Evesham Road in Redditch, serving homes and businesses across Worcestershire and the West Midlands.

Every job is done by hand in our own workshop. We’ll talk you through fabrics and finishes, give you a clear price up front, and treat your piece as if it were our own.
